Politique de confidentialité

Introduction

Seabyss is an online video game developed and operated by Seabyss. This privacy policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected, in accordance with Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information (Law 25).

Person Responsible for the Protection of Personal Information

Responsible entity: Seabyss

Email: privacy@seabyss.com

Personal Information Collected

As part of the use of the Seabyss launcher and game, the following personal information may be collected:

  • Email address
  • Player username (nickname)
  • Account identifiers
  • Gameplay and progression data
  • Technical data (IP address, connection logs, security-related data)

Seabyss does not collect or store any banking or payment information.

Purpose of Collection

Personal information is collected solely to:

  • Create and manage player accounts
  • Provide access to online services
  • Save game progress
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Manage digital content and in-game purchases
  • Improve game stability and user experience

Technologies and Third-Party Service Providers

Seabyss uses the following technologies and third-party services:

  • PlayFab (Microsoft) - account management, game data, and security
  • Unity - game engine
  • Mirror Networking - real-time multiplayer networking
  • Xsolla - payment processing

These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies and security standards.

Transfer of Personal Information Outside Quebec

Some personal information may be processed or stored outside Quebec or Canada, particularly through PlayFab and Xsolla services.

Before using these services, Seabyss conducted a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) to ensure that appropriate data protection measures are in place.

Retention of Personal Information

Personal information is retained:

  • As long as the player account remains active
  • Or as required by applicable legal obligations

Players may request the deletion of their account at any time.

User Rights

In accordance with Law 25, users have the right to:

  • Access their personal information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their account and data
  • Withdraw consent, subject to legal obligations

Requests must be sent to: privacy@seabyss.com

Minors

Seabyss is not intended for children under the age of 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from minors without the consent required by law.

Security and Privacy Incidents

Seabyss implements reasonable security measures, including:

  • Secure authentication systems
  • Restricted access to systems
  • Technical monitoring and logging

Any privacy incident involving personal information will be recorded in an internal incident log and handled in accordance with legal requirements.

Consent

By creating an account or using Seabyss, users acknowledge that they have read and accepted this privacy policy.

Modifications

This privacy policy may be updated at any time. Any changes take effect upon publication.
